About Me

I am a PhD student in Robotics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, based at the T Stone Robotics Institute. I work on robot manipulation, with a focus on 4D interaction world models, vision–language–action (VLA) models, and perception-guided grasping.

I am currently working on self-improving robotic systems. My experience spans 4D robot–object interaction generation and real-world robot deployment, including developing the vision and grasping algorithms for an industrial sorting system operating in Hong Kong.

WORLD MODELS

4D Robot–Object Interaction World Models

World-model workstream lead Aug 2025–present

We distill video diffusion priors into explicit 4D robot–object interactions without large-scale robot-specific 4D training data. A hybrid Gaussian representation couples deformable objects with articulated robots, preserving robot geometry and joint limits. Two-stage optimization first generates object and robot motion jointly, then refines robot trajectories to improve contact consistency and motion smoothness.

The generated interactions support multi-view rendering, cross-robot transfer, and policy-training data generation. We demonstrate the downstream use of this data by training diffusion policies for five real-world manipulation tasks.

3D REPRESENTATIONS / VLA

Object-Centric Semantic Fields

First-author research

A continuous, object-centric semantic representation that can be queried at explicit 3D locations. It provides functional-part cues for manipulation, connecting object geometry with where and how a robot should interact. My broader aim is to connect such object-centric representations with vision–language–action (VLA) policies.

PERCEPTION-GUIDED GRASPING

Hyperspectral Imaging for Robotic Grasping

First-author research RA-L 2025 · ROBIO 2024

PRISM enables compact hyperspectral scanning; SpectralGrasp combines spectral and spatial information to generate suction points. Together, they connect material perception with robotic grasping and textile sorting.
